3. `diy_competition.c::DIYLeagueTournamentMatchCommon` continua a chiamare l'originale `DIY_PlayOrSimulateLeagueFixture` (selector0=OG RESULT/CPU, selector2=OG MATCH). Un solo `Expanded_CompetitionCoordinator_Advance` possiede il tempo.
